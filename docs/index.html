If it’s almost prime time, it’s almost time for Almost Cable. The thing that’s much like cable, roughly speaking. Not from a cable company. From a folder.

*LocalCable is not cable. LocalCable is not a cable operator, a cable system, a cable service, a multiple-system operator, a headend, a set-top box, or a guy in a van. The word “Cable” appears in the name as a mood. If this were cable, the box would just say CABLE. The box does say Cable. That is the second half of a compound noun and our attorneys have asked us to stop talking. This product may look like a TV listings grid, behave like a TV listings grid, and hurt like nostalgia. Do not call it cable. Do not call your congressman. Do not call the cable guy; he will not know what this is. Contains no coaxial. May contain folders. If it starts getting hotter, that is not legally heat. If you can still legally call something cable after stapling a coaxial lead to a lasagna, that is still more cable than this. Come on.

LIVE FIELD REPORTS · LOCAL 3 NEWS

THE BUSINESS

IF IT PLAYS, IT LEADS

Playback joins at the guide’s current time, the way cable does, which this is not. A show that started at 4:00 starts twenty minutes in at 4:20. That is a live join. We are not calling it live in the FCC sense. Start over on the HUD plays from 0:00. Video plays in the page. Optional mpv on the server. Optional regret.

OVERNIGHT SHIFT

THE GRID NEVER STOPS

Real durations from ffprobe. A rolling window from now − 6 hours through now + 18, like a lineup, which this isn’t. Adjacent blocks abut. There are no commercial gaps unless you put commercials in the folder. If you put commercials in the folder, that is still not a commercial break in the legal sense. Come on.

PUBLIC ACCESS

YOUR FOLDER IS THE NETWORK

Classic channel folders still work. We are not saying they are channels in the ITU sense. A Jellyfin parent with Movies/ and Shows/ is mixed onto genre networks we invented — Nightfall, Thunderbolt, Chuckle, Starline, Toonbox, Prime, Local 8. Invented is the important word. No account. No cloud. No franchise agreement. Offline-first on Linux.

LATE NIGHT · PAID PROGRAMMING

PUT ON THE GLASSES

They Live hid the ads in the ads. Nightcrawler sold the footage back to the same city that produced it. LocalCable is the opposite trick: the guide is the product, the product is your own shelves, and the word cable is a vibe we are not prepared to defend in court.

Press S or the GLS key on the remote. The color goes. The billboards tell the truth, which is: this is not cable. It is meant to look like it.

INSTALL · TONIGHT

Python 3.11+ · uv or pip · ffmpeg / ffprobe · optional mpv

cd LocalCable
uv sync --extra dev
uv run localcable --media-root ~/Videos

Then open http://127.0.0.1:8787/ — or bind 0.0.0.0 and watch from another computer on the LAN.

README

As seen after sign-off. Not available in stores. We cannot send a man in a van. If it was cable, a man in a van would already be on the way, and he would be legally a cable man. Rewind is a courtesy.

Everything runs offline on Linux except optional keyless artwork lookups. One process serves the guide, the streams, and the schedule. Headed mode opens a browser. Headless mode is the same page on a box in the closet. It looks like a cable box. It’s meant to. If it was a cable box, it would just say CABLE BOX. It does not. Come on.
